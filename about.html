About

Ellie's story

A little about how I got here, what I'm drawn to, and where to find me when I'm not designing.

Traveling, searching for inspiration all around the world

I'm a full-stack developer turned UX designer. I unexpectedly landed on BYU-Idaho's UX team and fell in love with intentional design.

Because I come from development, I speak both languages, so I can work just as easily with designers as with the developers who build what we design.

The best designs are the ones where people were remembered every step of the way.

As the child of a minimalist mom and a designer dad, I'm quite literally the love child of functional flows and beautiful visuals. I believe the best design is the kind people never even notice. It just lets them do the thing they came to do.
